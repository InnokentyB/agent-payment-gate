How it works

  1. Didit confirms an existing verification through an authenticated server read.
  2. The demo owner signs a wallet challenge and separately reviews and signs exact spending terms.
  3. An OpenZeppelin smart account, verifier, rolling spending policy and recipient/per-payment policy enforce the rule on Soroban. The Stellar SDK submits and reconciles native XLM transfers.
  4. A durable local invoice journal saves the signed transaction before submission. Unknown outcomes are checked by their original hash; the owner can remove the on-chain rule.

Scope and disclosure

Single-owner prototype with server-held demo keys. No mainnet, fiat gateway, autonomous LLM purchasing agent, global biometric-uniqueness or production-readiness claim. Didit document images and biometric templates are not placed on-chain. Reusable KYC was not exercised. Local invoice idempotency does not imply global on-chain invoice uniqueness.
